The Problem

Supply Chains Are Flying Blind During Disruptions

India's Rs 14 trillion domestic freight market operates with no automated disruption intelligence layer.

-Reactive, Not Proactive: A dispatcher typically learns about a highway closure from a phone call or WhatsApp, not from a system that has already correlated it against every active shipment through that corridor.
-Cold Chain Disasters: Temperature excursions on vaccine / pharma shipments worth Rs 50L+ are only discovered at delivery when cargo is already spoiled. There is no pre-delivery detection.
-Fleet Sitting Idle: Trucks sit in low-demand depots while adjacent corridors face surge capacity. Manual matching takes hours. Fleet utilisation stays below 65%.
-No Carrier Comparison: Reroute decisions are made without price, ETA, and reliability comparison. Dispatchers fly blind on trade-offs that cost the business lakhs.

Our Solution

RouteGuard AI - Five-Stage Logistics Control Tower

Deterministic math engines + IBM BoB grounded AI copilot = auditable, trustworthy logistics intelligence.

1. Live Disruption Radar

Integrates real-time GDACS disaster/weather API + NHAI highway advisories. Correlates them against active shipment coordinates using Haversine geometry to find which shipments are actually at risk.

2. Cold Chain IoT Monitor

Ingests temperature sensor streams before delivery. Calculates degree-hours out of range, excursion duration, and classifies severity against WHO TRS 961 and FDA 21 CFR GDP standards.

3. CrewAI Carrier Auction

Three autonomous carrier agents (Speed, Cost, Reliability) each independently propose a bid grounded in the reroute deltas. A Negotiator agent selects the best bid for the stated priority.

4. Fleet Redeployment Engine

Scans idle fleet assets within a 100km geo-radius of disruption nodes. Ranks and matches them to overload corridors by capacity, reefer suitability, and proximity.

Deep Dive: Risk Engine

Deterministic 0-100 Risk Scoring - Fully Auditable

Every point on the risk score traces to a named, inspectable RiskFactor object. No black box.

Factor: Disruption Severity

LOW=10pts, MODERATE=25pts, HIGH=40pts, CRITICAL=55pts. Worst intersecting disruption wins (cascading does not add linearly).

Factor: Cargo Sensitivity

STANDARD=0pts, HIGH_VALUE=10pts, TIME_CRITICAL=15pts, COLD_CHAIN=20pts

Factor: Cargo Value (INR)

>= Rs 50L gives +15pts | >= Rs 10L gives +8pts | Below threshold gives 0pts

Example: SHP-1001 - Score 85/100

disruption_severity = 55 pts (CRITICAL: NH-48 monsoon flood)
cargo_sensitivity = 20 pts (COLD_CHAIN vaccines)
cargo_value = 15 pts (Rs 65L >= Rs 50L threshold)
compounding = 0 pts (single disruption)
Total: 90/100 capped at 85 - band: CRITICAL

IBM BoB Copilot reads this exact breakdown and cites only these numbers in its brief.

Deep Dive: Cold Chain

Pre-Delivery IoT Excursion Detection & Regulatory Classification

SHP-1001 Vaccine Shipment - 6-Hour Sensor Log Analysis

Sensor Telemetry Parsed

7 IoT readings over 6h, Avg: 6.4 deg C, Min: 4.1 deg C, Max: 10.4 deg C
Door open events: 1, Battery: 72% (low, flagged)

Excursion Detected

Duration: 120 minutes above 8 deg C threshold
Degree-hours out of range: 4.4 DH
Peak temperature: 10.4 deg C

Regulatory Classification Logic

NOMINAL: 0 min excursion
STABILITY_OK: <=15 min AND max<=10 deg C AND min>=1 deg C
QUARANTINE_REQUIRED: <=120 min AND max<=15 deg C AND min>=0 deg C
CARGO_SPOILED: >120 min OR max>15 deg C

Verdict: QUARANTINE_REQUIRED

Standard: WHO TRS 961 / FDA 21 CFR GDP Guidelines

"Moderate excursion detected. Automatic quarantine flag added.
QA stability review and data log download required at receiving dock."

This alert fires before delivery, while there is still time to:
- Reroute to nearest temperature-controlled facility
- Notify QA team and insurance
- Dispatch replacement stock pre-emptively

Without RouteGuard AI: breach discovered on arrival. Rs 65L cargo spoiled. Full replacement cost + liability.

IBM BoB Integration

IBM BoB / watsonx.ai - Genuinely Load-Bearing AI

Not AI sprinkled on top. The LLM solves a real problem that deterministic code cannot: natural-language synthesis under grounding constraints.

The Grounding Architecture

The system prompt sent to Granite explicitly says: "Cite ONLY numbers present in the payload. Never invent a price, ETA, distance, or risk score that is not in the JSON you were given."

This single instruction is why a dispatcher can trust the output.

What BoB Actually Does

Receives a JSON payload of already-computed facts: risk score + factors, reroute options, fleet candidates, cold chain report. Synthesises them into a single, readable dispatcher brief with one clear recommended action.

CrewAI Multi-Agent Negotiation

Three carrier agents (Swift, ValueHaul, TrustLine) with distinct business personas independently bid on a reroute option. A fourth Negotiator agent picks the best bid for the stated priority.

Sample BoB Dispatcher Brief (grounded)
// POST /api/copilot/brief { "shipment_id": "SHP-1001" }
"SHP-1001 (Vaccines, Rs65L) currently scores 85/100 CRITICAL. The dominant factor is a CRITICAL-rated monsoon flood on NH-48 (+55pts), compounded by cold-chain sensitivity (+20pts) and high cargo value (+15pts).
The cold chain sensor log shows a 10.4 deg C excursion for 120 minutes classified QUARANTINE_REQUIRED under WHO TRS 961.
RECOMMENDED: Bypass via NH48 alternate carriageway. Delta: +3.5h, +Rs2,200. Projected risk after reroute: 38/100."
