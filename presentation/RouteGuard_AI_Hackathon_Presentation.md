## Slide 2: The Problem: Supply Chain Fragility
- **Cascading Vulnerability:** Weather events, port strikes, and highway blockades disrupt hundreds of active shipments silently.
- **Cold Chain Risk:** $500K+ vaccine/pharma shipments spoil due to unmonitored temperature breaches discovered only at delivery.
- **Unoptimized Fleet:** Idle trucks sit in low-demand hubs while adjacent lanes face surge capacity bottlenecks.
- **Manual Reactive Chaos:** Dispatchers rely on spreadsheets, phone calls, and WhatsApp groups rather than automated correlation.

---

## Slide 3: The RouteGuard AI Solution
RouteGuard AI delivers an end-to-end AI logistics control tower with four integrated pillars:
1. **Live Disruption Radar:** Correlates real GDACS weather alerts & NHAI highway advisories against active shipment coordinates.
2. **Cold Chain IoT Inspector:** Pre-delivery sensor stream monitoring with WHO/FDA regulatory severity classification.
3. **Multi-Agent Carrier Auction:** CrewAI carrier agents (Speed vs. Cost vs. Reliability) negotiate optimal reroute bids.
4. **Fleet Redeployment Engine:** Matches idle trucks/containers to overload freight corridors in real time.

---

## Slide 4: System Architecture & Hybrid AI Pattern
- **Deterministic Risk Core:** 0–100 point auditable risk engine. Every point traces to an inspectable factor (Proximity, Cargo Sensitivity, Delay Duration).
- **Multi-Agent CrewAI Layer:** Autonomous carrier bidding personas simulating real-world rate & ETA trade-offs.
- **IBM BoB / Watsonx Grounded Copilot:** Synthesizes complex engine outputs into zero-hallucination dispatcher briefs.
- **Tech Stack:** Python, FastAPI, CrewAI, GDACS API, HTML5/CSS3 HSL Glassmorphism Control Tower.

---

## Slide 5: Deep Dive: Cold Chain IoT Telemetry & Regulatory Guardrails
- **Continuous Sensor Logging:** Ingests live temperature & humidity streams every hour.
- **Pre-Delivery Breach Alerting:** Identifies temperature spikes (e.g. 9.4°C breach) *before* cargo arrives at destination.
- **Regulatory Severity Classification:** Automatically tags breaches against **WHO Vaccine Cold Chain Protocols** and **USFDA 21 CFR Part 11**.
- **Actionable Intervention:** Instant emergency reroute dispatch to nearest refrigerated cold-storage facility.

---

## Slide 6: Multi-Agent Carrier Negotiation (CrewAI)
- **FastFreight Express Agent:** Prioritizes speed and maximum time saved (-4.5 hours).
- **EcoCarrier Logistics Agent:** Prioritizes cost savings (lowest rate delta).
- **Reliant Cold Logistics Agent:** Prioritizes SLA compliance and cold-storage compliance (99.5% reliability).
- **Negotiator Agent:** Selects winning bid based on dispatcher priority rules in real time.

## Slide 7: Fleet Utilisation & Idle Asset Matching
- **Geo-Proximity Engine:** Scans idle fleet inventory within a 100km radius of disruption nodes.
- **Asset Match Matrix:** Ranks available trucks based on capacity, refrigeration suitability, and driver availability.
- **Redeployment Efficiency:** Boosts overall fleet utilization from <65% to >90%.
