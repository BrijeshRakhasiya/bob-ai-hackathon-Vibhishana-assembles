Industry Challenge

The Logistics Disruption Crisis

  • Reactive Chaos: Monsoon flooding, highway closures, and port strikes disrupt freight with zero automated early warning.
  • Cold Chain Disasters: Temperature excursions spoil ₹50L+ vaccine cargoes — breaches are only discovered at delivery when it is too late.
  • Wasted Assets: Trucks sit idle in low-demand depots while adjacent freight corridors face severe capacity bottlenecks.
  • No Carrier Comparison: Dispatchers make split-second rerouting choices without price, speed, or reliability trade-off data.
Core Solution

The RouteGuard AI Control Tower

1. Live Disruption Radar

GDACS weather feeds & NHAI advisories correlated with active shipment routes using Haversine geometry.

2. Cold Chain IoT Monitor

Pre-delivery telemetry parsing with WHO & USFDA 21 CFR regulatory severity classification.

3. CrewAI Carrier Auction

Multi-agent carrier bidding comparing Speed, Cost, and Reliability personas in real time.

4. Fleet Redeployment

Idle asset locator matching unassigned trucks near disruptions to surge freight corridors.

Architecture

Deterministic Core + IBM BoB Grounded Copilot

  • 0–100 Risk Engine: Fully deterministic score. Every point traces to an explicit factor (Disruption Proximity, Cargo Sensitivity, Delay Delta).
  • Zero Hallucination Guarantee: IBM BoB Copilot is strictly bounded to synthesize numbers already computed by the math engine.
  • CrewAI Agent Integration: Autonomous carrier bidding personas negotiate rates and ETA deltas automatically.
